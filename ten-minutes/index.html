Free Guided Practice

Ten Minutes That
Are Only Yours

A guided practice for the woman who takes care of everyone.

Nobody needs anything from you for the length of this recording. You do not have to fix anything, figure anything out, or do any of it correctly.

What You’ll Receive

A Guided Audio Practice You Can Keep

Enter your name and email, and it starts playing on the next page. You can save it to your phone and keep it.

  • A guided audio practice you can listen to anywhere, as many times as you want. It is yours to keep.
  • Ten minutes of the actual work I do with women: settling into your body, noticing the part of you that holds everything together, and finding the part that has been waiting a long time to put it down.
  • You do not need to be artistic, you do not need to know what is wrong, and you do not need to be ready for anything.

You will want about fifteen minutes, somewhere you can close a door, and a piece of paper.

First

Arriving

“I never actually stop.”

Shoes off, feet on the floor, letting something else hold your weight for a few minutes. This part is smaller than it sounds and harder than it sounds.

Then

The one who holds it together

“Everyone thinks I have it together.”

We find where she lives in your body. She is not the problem, and we are not going to try to get rid of her. We are going to thank her.

Last

The one who wants to stop

“I do not remember the last time I played.”

The part of you that is not managing anything and not useful to anyone. She may be very quiet. We look for her anyway.

Alynne Davis, LCMHC, LPC I’m a Licensed Clinical Mental Health Counselor and Expressive Arts Therapist in Charlotte, North Carolina. I have spent over a decade working with women on eating disorders, body image, trauma and ADHD, and I have my own history with all of it. This recording is a small piece of what that work actually feels like.
