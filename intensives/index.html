Therapeutic Intensives in Charlotte, NC

For women ready to stop circling the surface. An intensive gives the work hours instead of minutes, so what has been waiting finally has room.

Finding the Right Path for You

There are many ways to do meaningful inner work, and the support you need may look different depending on the season of life you are in.

Therapeutic intensives are focused psychotherapy. They are for women who want to address a mental health concern, most often an eating disorder, binge eating, or body image, and sometimes trauma or relational wounds, in longer, deeper blocks of time than a weekly session allows.

Looking for the Embodied Reclamation Series?

The Embodied Foundations Series and Embodied Self-Discovery Series are coaching, not therapy. They are a nonclinical path to self-discovery, personal growth, and embodied living, offered through the Embodied Reclamation Studio. Explore the Series.

Therapeutic Intensives

For when food and your body are where it shows

You know the pattern. You could probably explain it better than most people. The evening binge after a day of holding it together. The mirror that decides what kind of day it will be. The recovery that keeps stalling in the same place, no matter how well you understand why.

Understanding was never the missing piece. What drives binge eating and body image struggles lives underneath insight: in shame, in the body, in parts of you that learned food was the one reliable place to find relief or control. A 50-minute session often ends just as we reach it.

An intensive gives that work room. We can go into it, and still come back out, settled, before you leave.

1.5 to 2 Hours

Extended Sessions

Time to go into the shame, the grief, or the anger underneath the eating, and still leave grounded. Extended sessions can also be scheduled more often than weekly when you need more support than a weekly rhythm allows.

  • Body image that takes up more of your life than you want it to
  • Feeling stuck in recovery, or sliding back whenever stress rises
  • ADHD and trauma tangled up with how you eat

What an Intensive Can Focus On

  • Eating disorders and body image, the center of my work
  • Trauma and attachment, including the patterns a hard relationship left behind
  • Emotional and relational healing, when you keep returning to the same place with the people closest to you

How We Begin

Intensives start with care, not a booking. First, we meet for an initial assessment, so I understand your history and what is happening now. For eating disorder work, next comes medical clearance from your physician, because an eating disorder can affect the body in ways that are not always visible. Then we decide together whether an intensive is the right fit, or whether weekly therapy or a different level of care would serve you better right now.

Intensives are outpatient care. If you need more support than outpatient work can safely offer, I will help you find the right next step. You can read more about how I work with eating disorders.

Alynne Davis, LCMHC offers therapeutic intensives, including extended sessions and half-day intensives, in person in Charlotte, NC and via telehealth throughout NC and SC, including Raleigh, Asheville, Durham, Wilmington, Columbia SC, and Greenville SC.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a therapeutic intensive and the Embodied Reclamation Series?

They serve different purposes. A therapeutic intensive is psychotherapy, for addressing a mental health concern such as an eating disorder, body image, or trauma. The Embodied Reclamation Series is coaching, a nonclinical path to self-discovery and personal growth. Both honor the connection between mind, body, and lived experience. If you are not sure which fits, we can work it out together on a free consultation.

Are intensives available in person or online?

Both. Intensives are offered in person at my Charlotte, NC office and via telehealth for women located anywhere in North Carolina and South Carolina. Scheduling is flexible and arranged together around your availability.

How do therapeutic intensives begin?

They start with care, not a booking. First comes an initial assessment of your history and what is happening now, then medical clearance from your physician, and then we decide together whether an intensive is the right fit or whether weekly therapy or a different level of care would serve you better.

Can I do an intensive if I am already in weekly therapy?

Yes. Many women come to an intensive while in weekly therapy, with me or with someone else, because they want more focused time than a 50-minute session allows.
