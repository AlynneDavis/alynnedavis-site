Therapy Intensives in Charlotte, NC

For women who don’t want to spend months circling the surface. Intensives create the time and space to move through what feels stuck, far more quickly than weekly therapy alone.

The Embodied Self-Discovery Series

A Guided Journey to Remember, Return, and Reclaim Yourself

The Embodied Self-Discovery Series is a personalized, assessment-informed experience that brings together psychological exploration, the MARI® assessment, parts work, somatic awareness, expressive arts, and guided reflection.

Through approximately 6.5 hours of individual sessions, we explore the experiences, beliefs, protective patterns, and parts of yourself that have shaped who you are and how you move through the world.

Throughout the series, you’ll receive a companion journal designed to support reflection, deepen self-awareness, and help you integrate your discoveries beyond our time together.

This isn’t simply about understanding your story. It’s about creating opportunities to reconnect with yourself, explore new perspectives, and begin making choices that align with your authentic needs, values, and aspirations.

Grounded in the RRR Method™ of Remember, Return, and Reclaim, the series creates space for compassionate self-discovery through both reflection and lived experience.

Your Experience Includes

  • A comprehensive 90-minute initial assessment and exploration
  • A 60-minute MARI® assessment experience
  • A two-hour collaborative review of findings and internal parts mapping
  • A two-hour personalized experiential exploration and integration session
  • A companion self-discovery journal to support the process between sessions and beyond

The goal isn’t to become someone different. It’s to deepen your relationship with who you are.

About the MARI® Assessment

The MARI® (Mandala Assessment Research Instrument) is a clinically validated assessment tool that uses creative art expression and symbolic imagery to access parts of your inner world that words alone may not reach. It offers a non-verbal pathway to self-understanding. No artistic skill is needed, only a willingness to explore.

Included With the Series

Your Companion Self-Discovery Journal

Designed to support reflection, deepen self-awareness, and help you integrate your discoveries between sessions and long after the series ends.

Who Is This For?

The Embodied Self-Discovery Series may be a good fit if you:

  • Want a comprehensive understanding of your emotional and relational patterns
  • Are curious about non-verbal, creative approaches to self-understanding
  • Have been in therapy before but feel like something deeper hasn't been addressed
  • Are navigating a major life transition and need clarity
  • Struggle with perfectionism, self-doubt, or disconnection from yourself
  • Want focused, uninterrupted time for this work rather than working through it week by week
  • Are a clinician or helping professional seeking your own deep personal work

Details

  • Format: 4 individual sessions, approximately 6.5 hours in total
  • Scheduling: Flexible, arranged together around your availability
  • Location: In-person in Charlotte, NC or virtual
  • Includes: 90-minute initial assessment and exploration, 60-minute MARI® assessment, two-hour review of findings and parts mapping, two-hour experiential exploration and integration session, and a companion self-discovery journal
  • Led by: Alynne Davis, MA, LCMHC

Alynne Davis, LCMHC offers therapy intensives, extended sessions and half-day intensives in person in Charlotte, NC and via telehealth throughout NC and SC, including Raleigh, Asheville, Durham, Wilmington, Columbia SC, and Greenville SC.
