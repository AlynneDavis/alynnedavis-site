Letters from the Studio

Writing on embodiment, self-abandonment, and the long way back to yourself. Not advice. Just what I am noticing, thinking through, and living.

Letter One

What Is an Embodied Reclamation?

The clay figures, the afternoon everything broke open, and the question that has shaped fifteen years of work. Healing as remembering rather than fixing.

Letter Two

The First Relationship We Leave

On self-abandonment, empathy, and the quiet ways we learn to leave ourselves in order to belong. What if embodiment is not something we have to learn, but something we slowly forget?

Letter Three

The Person Who Stays

On self-presence, and the many quiet ways we leave ourselves. Healing is not measured by whether you have difficult mornings, but by whether you abandon yourself when you do.

Letter Four

When Being Human Feels Like Failure

On capacity, dependability, and the calendar that once meant freedom and slowly became a cage. What if you have not lost yourself, only the capacity to keep performing constant competence?

Where these begin

Letters arrive first by email

Each letter is written and sent from Letters from Embodied Reclamation. A few land here afterward. If you would rather they come to you, that is where to find them.
