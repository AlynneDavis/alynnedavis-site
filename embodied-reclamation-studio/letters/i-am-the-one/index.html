Art from the Studio

I Am the One

A digital collage, and the words that came when I let her speak.

I made this collage and wrote these words inside the Embodied Leader Mastermind through Healing Embodied, a space where I do my own healing work. Our mentor asked us to create, or move, with the one who holds our purpose, and then let her speak to us. So I let her speak. Something clicked and settled in me. I understood what my body of work needed to be, and who it is meant for. I felt lighter, and clearer.

I am the one who battled the storms and bears the scars hidden so deep you cannot see them from the outside.

I am the one who loves so deeply that it pains me to see it, in the reflection of the face of another.

I am the one who questioned her worth and lost the way back to herself, but knew it deep within her soul.

I am the warrior who kept getting up, time and again, after being pushed and put down with every word that you flung at her.

I am the one who ached with grief for the hopes that had been tarnished by the fraudulent love that you claimed you held for me.

I am the one who placed others before herself so often that she withered, as she tarried for so long in the shadows without the sunlight or water to nourish her heart and her body.

I am the one who accepted less than the best in order to keep the peace and survive the emotional whippings and toxic consumption of cowardly pain and self-loathing.

I am the one who showed up over and over without question or resentment, because to love was to sacrifice the needs of oneself, or so she thought, due to the narratives of shame that informed her.

I am the one who was a delicate butterfly, full of beauty and hope, that you tried to trap in the jar to suffocate, slowly, with every exhale you inhaled.

I am the feminine that sacrificed her pleasure to please your desires, in order to survive the attacks and keep the connection.

I am the one who rose up and chose different, little by little, clawing and climbing back to her.

I am the one who remembered who she was before she believed that she was unworthy and had to earn her value.

I am the one who returned to herself through the connection to her Creator and creations, and I am the one who reclaimed her life, her love, her body and her essence, fiercely and wildly.

Remember, Return, Reclaim

She remembered. She returned. She reclaimed.

Those three movements are the heart of everything I do. If something in her words felt like your own, you can read more about the RRR Method™, or spend some time with the other pieces from the Studio.
