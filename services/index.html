Holistic Therapy for Women in Charlotte, NC

A steady, weekly place to do the deeper work. In person in Charlotte, or online across North Carolina and South Carolina. Explore the areas of support I offer and find the path that feels right for you.

How We Can Work Together

Many women come to therapy feeling disconnected from themselves, overwhelmed by anxiety or perfectionism, or stuck in patterns around food, control, or relationships. My work blends somatic and nervous system practices, creative expression, and deep, compassionate conversation to help you rebuild trust with yourself from the inside out.

That means the work happens in your body, not only in your head. And you do not need to be artistic. Creative expression here simply means finding ways to reach what words alone cannot.

My approach is also holistic, which means I look at your whole life rather than a diagnosis. Sleep, nourishment, movement, relationships, spirituality, self-care, and the environment you live in all shape how you feel, and none of that shows up on an intake form. We pay attention to all of it, because you are a whole person, not a set of symptoms.

Whether you are seeking weekly therapy, a focused healing intensive, or coaching, we can create a path forward that meets you where you are.

Session Details and Logistics

In-Person and Virtual Options

I offer both in-person sessions in Charlotte, NC and virtual therapy sessions for clients located anywhere in North Carolina and South Carolina. Virtual sessions are conducted through a secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth platform, providing the same quality of care from the comfort of your own space.

Fees & Payment

This is primarily a private-pay practice. This allows for a therapeutic relationship that isn’t shaped by what insurance will or won’t cover, and for the kind of deep, unhurried work that is at the heart of this practice.

I accept payment via credit card, debit card, and HSA/FSA cards. Payment is due at the time of each session.

Why Private Pay Supports Better Care

When therapy is paid privately rather than routed through insurance, something important shifts. The focus stays entirely on you and what you actually need, rather than on what a managed care panel will approve.

  • Your care isn’t limited by a diagnosis. Insurance requires a mental health diagnosis to justify treatment, and that diagnosis becomes a permanent part of your medical record. Private pay means we can do this work without attaching a label to you.
  • You decide how long we work together. Insurance plans dictate session limits, treatment length, and sometimes even the type of therapy you can receive. Private pay means your healing timeline is ours to determine together, not an insurance company’s.
  • Your privacy is protected. When insurance is involved, your records, diagnosis, and treatment details are accessible to your insurance company. With private pay, what happens in our sessions stays between us.
  • The full depth of care is available to you. I’m able to use the approaches that are actually right for you, whether that is expressive arts therapy, somatic work, or IFS, without needing to justify each one to a third party.
  • Extended and intensive sessions are possible. Because I’m not bound by insurance session formats, I’m able to offer extended sessions and half-day intensives for clients who want to go deeper, faster.

If cost is a concern, superbills are available after each session so you can seek out-of-network reimbursement from your insurance company. A limited number of sliding scale spots are also available by application, and you’re welcome to ask about availability during your free consultation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know which therapy service is right for me?

Many clients begin with a free consultation so we can talk about what you are experiencing and determine the most supportive approach. Whether that involves weekly therapy, expressive arts sessions, or a focused intensive, we will decide together what best supports your needs.

Do I need to have a diagnosis to start therapy?

No diagnosis is required to begin therapy. Many women seek support simply because they feel overwhelmed, disconnected from themselves, or stuck in patterns that are no longer working.

Do you offer therapy in person or online?

I offer both in-person therapy at my Charlotte, North Carolina office and secure virtual sessions for clients located anywhere in North Carolina and South Carolina.

Do you accept insurance?

This is primarily a private-pay practice. A limited number of insurance plans are accepted. Please reach out to ask about your specific plan. A superbill is provided after each session for potential out-of-network reimbursement, and you can use Mentaya to check your benefits and file claims easily. A limited number of sliding scale spots are also available by application.
