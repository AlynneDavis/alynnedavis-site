Embodied Reclamation Institute  ·  1:1 Coaching

Embodied Reclamation Coaching

Coaching for women ready to come home to themselves — to remember who they are, return to themselves, and reclaim the parts of life that survival, shame, or expectation pushed aside.

Schedule a Discovery Call

You may have built a life that looks capable from the outside.

You keep going. You care for other people. You meet expectations. You think deeply about your patterns and may already understand why you struggle. And yet, something still feels distant.

Perhaps you feel disconnected from your body, uncertain of your own voice, exhausted by perfectionism, or caught between who you have learned to be and who you sense you are becoming.

Coaching offers a space to slow down, listen beneath the noise, and reconnect with what has been waiting for your attention.

This work is not about fixing you. It is about helping you remember who you are, return to yourself, and reclaim the parts of your life that survival, shame, responsibility, or cultural expectations may have pushed aside.

How I Support Women

A more authentic, embodied way of living.

I work with women navigating a major transition, questioning an old identity, rebuilding trust with themselves, or simply realizing that the way they have always lived no longer feels sustainable. Our work together may support you in…

Reconnecting With Your Authentic Self

When you have spent years adapting to what others need, your own voice can be hard to hear. Together we explore who you are beneath roles, expectations, and protective patterns — and what you want your life to reflect now.

Moving Beyond Perfectionism & Overfunctioning

Perfectionism often begins as protection, but over time it exhausts. We develop a more compassionate relationship with achievement and make room for rest, creativity, experimentation, and enoughness.

Building Self-Trust

Through reflective conversation, body awareness, creative exploration, and practical experimentation, you strengthen your ability to recognize your needs, make aligned decisions, and trust the wisdom developing within you.

Reclaiming Your Voice & Boundaries

We explore the deeper patterns beneath people-pleasing, conflict avoidance, and self-silencing — so you can honor your limits and stay connected to yourself in relationships.

Navigating Life Transitions

Motherhood, divorce, career change, caregiving, grief, an empty nest, midlife — a place to make meaning of what is ending, listen for what is emerging, and move forward without rushing into premature certainty.

Creating a More Embodied Life

Sometimes the mind understands what needs to change before the rest of us catches up. We learn the language of your body and nervous system with compassion — letting mind, body, emotion, and lived experience participate in your choices.

Reconnecting With Creativity & Play

Creativity is one of the ways we discover what we feel and imagine new possibilities. Our work may include writing, imagery, metaphor, movement, mindfulness, or nature-based reflection. No artistic experience is needed.

Clarifying Purpose & Direction

When something feels restless or unclear, we listen to that longing without forcing it into an immediate plan — exploring your values, gifts, and desires so your next steps emerge from alignment rather than pressure.

A More Compassionate Relationship With Yourself

Many women have learned to motivate themselves through criticism. We notice the inner voices shaping your life, understand where they came from, and cultivate a steadier, more respectful way of relating to yourself.

Turning Insight Into Lived Change

You may already understand your patterns — you have read the books and done the work. Coaching bridges the space between knowing and practicing, translating awareness into embodied experiments and small acts of reclamation.

The Embodied Reclamation Approach

Remember. Return. Reclaim.

My coaching is grounded in the RRR Method™ — a framework for moving through awareness, embodiment, and integration at your own pace. The goal is not to become a better-performing version of yourself. It is to become more fully yourself.

Phase 01

Remember

We begin by remembering what has always been true beneath shame, survival, and expectation. Your worth. Your resilience. Your voice. Your creativity. Your capacity to know yourself.

Phase 02

Return

We return to the body, curiosity, emotional experience, nervous system awareness, creativity, presence, and relationship. This is where insight begins to become lived experience.

Phase 03

Reclaim

From that grounded place, you can reclaim agency, boundaries, joy, purpose, play, authenticity, and the freedom to participate more fully in your own life.

What Our Work May Include

Thoughtful, collaborative, and responsive to you.

Our sessions are shaped by what you need. We may draw on reflective conversation, values exploration, somatic awareness, mindfulness, expressive arts, journaling, metaphor, creative practices, nervous system education, practical goal-setting, and between-session experiments.

Some sessions may help you name what has been difficult to articulate. Others may help you make a decision, prepare for a conversation, explore a pattern, reconnect with desire, or take one grounded step toward a life that feels more like your own.

You will not be given a rigid formula for who you should become. You will be supported in listening more deeply to who you already are.

How We Begin

A Different Kind of Beginning

You do not need to have everything figured out before beginning. You may only know that something in you is asking for more honesty, more space, or a different way of living. That quiet knowing is enough to begin.

Begin With a Discovery Call

Every coaching relationship starts with a relaxed, no-pressure conversation — a chance to explore what you are longing for and whether coaching is the right support for you right now.

A Self-Discovery Assessment

Your first session includes a reflective assessment of where you are in your reclamation journey — so our work begins from a clear, honest picture of you and what matters most.

Customized to You

Coaching is shaped around your needs and pace. Some women work together over three months, others over six. Some include text support between sessions. There is no rigid curriculum — the container is built around you.

May Begin as an Intensive

For some women, this work begins with a Self-Discovery Intensive — a focused, deeper experience that can open into ongoing coaching when you are ready.

Because every path looks a little different, investment and structure are discussed together on your discovery call, once we understand what kind of support fits where you are.

This May Be a Good Fit

Coaching may be a good fit if you are…

  • Feeling disconnected from yourself
  • Moving through a transition or identity shift
  • Struggling with perfectionism, people-pleasing, or overfunctioning
  • Longing for more confidence, clarity, creativity, or purpose
  • Learning to trust your voice and honor your boundaries
  • Seeking support in turning self-awareness into meaningful change
  • Ready to explore your life with curiosity, honesty, and compassion

Clinical Depth. Coaching Container.

Coaching and Therapy

Coaching is not psychotherapy and does not replace mental health treatment. Therapy often focuses on diagnosing and treating mental health concerns, processing trauma, and addressing significant emotional distress. Coaching is generally oriented toward present-day growth, values, goals, decision-making, identity, and the creation of meaningful change.

Because I am also a licensed mental health professional, I take the distinction between therapy and coaching seriously. You will be working with someone who holds:

  • Licensure as a Clinical Mental Health Counselor (LCMHC, LPC)
  • Training as an Expressive Arts Therapist
  • Certification as a MARI Practitioner
  • Certification as a Nutritionist
  • Healing Embodied Practitioner certification (in progress — international)

That depth means this work is nuanced, body-aware, and held with genuine care — while remaining clearly within a coaching container. On our discovery call, we will discuss the scope of our work and whether coaching is the appropriate form of support for you.

Please note: Coaching is not therapy and does not constitute mental health treatment. If you are seeking therapy services in North Carolina or South Carolina, please visit Alynne Davis, PLLC. If you are in crisis, please call or text 988.

In the Spirit of Honesty

Who This Is Not For

Embodied Reclamation Coaching is a coaching relationship, not clinical treatment. It is not appropriate if you are currently in acute crisis, require medical monitoring, need psychiatric support, or are seeking a clinical diagnosis or formal treatment.

If you are unsure whether coaching or therapy is the right next step for where you are right now, I’m happy to explore that with you on a discovery call — no obligation, just a conversation.

An Invitation

You are not broken.

The parts of you that learned to please, perfect, protect, achieve, withdraw, or stay small were trying to help you survive and belong.

Remember

Return
