About the intensive

An intensive gives us several uninterrupted hours instead of a therapy hour. That length is what makes deeper experiential, somatic, expressive arts, trauma, and pattern-focused work possible, because we are not stopping just as something begins to open.

About the assessment

There are two different things people mean by this, and they go to two different places. If you are starting therapy with me, assessment is already part of how we begin, and we do that work in the first session rather than as a separate purchase. If what you need is a formal diagnosis on paper, for a prescriber or for accommodations, that is a different process and I can point you straight to it without you waiting on a call with me.

For that, the fastest route is the online diagnostic assessment I partner with. It is done at your own pace, there is no waitlist, and it produces the documentation a prescriber or an employer will actually accept. You can read about it and start on the diagnostic assessments page without needing to speak to me first. Send this form anyway if you would like me involved, or if you are also thinking about therapy.

That is the kind of understanding therapy is built for, and it is work we would do together rather than something you buy separately. You are welcome to send this as it is, and in my reply I will tell you honestly whether therapy or a formal assessment makes more sense for what you are after.

What the consultation is for

The free consultation is a short call to work out whether my expertise, approach, availability, and services line up with what you are looking for. It is not a therapy session or a full clinical assessment, and that is on purpose. The real work deserves more room than a 15-minute call can give it.

What the first call is for

This one is a discovery call rather than a consultation, and the difference matters. There is nothing clinical about it and nothing being assessed. It is a chance for me to hear where you are, what you are building, and what is in the way, and for you to get a feel for how I work before you commit to anything.

The discovery call is free. After that, practice consultation runs as a package rather than one-off sessions. The core is three working sessions of 60 to 90 minutes, workbooks to take you through the thinking in between, and email support so that you are not sitting on a question for three weeks waiting for the next call.

If you want more than that, further one-to-one sessions can be added, and for someone rebuilding something larger a longer engagement is possible. We would work out the right shape on the call. It is priced separately from case consultation, because it is different work, and it can be paid over instalments.
