Speaking, Workshops & Groups

For the people who hold others up. I bring workshops, talks and groups on body image, eating disorders, trauma, shame and burnout to schools, sororities, nonprofits, treatment programs and the caregivers who are running on empty.

Ask About Bringing Me In

What Happens in the Room

Most of the women I have met in workshops already know the facts. They have heard that diets do not work, that trauma lives in the body, that they should take care of themselves. What they have rarely had is a space where they can feel it land.

That is what I try to create. Every workshop combines clear, grounded education with something experiential: a reflective exercise, a creative prompt, a moment of noticing what is happening in your body. People leave understanding something new about themselves, not just holding more information.

I am a Licensed Clinical Mental Health Counselor specializing in eating disorders, body image and trauma, with a background in expressive arts and somatic work. The same philosophy that guides my practice, the RRR Method™, shapes how I teach: nothing is wrong with you, and the patterns that hurt you were once ways you learned to survive.

Ways to Work Together

Every format is shaped around your group, your setting and what the people in the room are carrying.

Schools, Sororities & Events

Workshops & Talks

Single sessions for student groups, sorority chapters, schools, NAMI programs and mental health awareness events. Education that feels human, with time to reflect and ask the questions people usually keep to themselves.

Nonprofits & Treatment Programs

Groups

Ongoing or short-run groups for treatment programs, recovery settings and community organizations, including groups I have led in substance use treatment programs. Built around safety, the nervous system and coming back into relationship with yourself.

Residency Programs & Helping Professions

Caring for the Caregivers

Support for the people who spend their days holding everyone else’s pain. I have led a support group for medical residents carrying the trauma of the work, and I offer the same kind of space for nurses, hospice teams and other helping professionals.

Topics

These are the themes I return to most. Each can be shaped for a one-hour talk, a longer workshop or an ongoing group.

  • Body image and the pressure to shrink. Why so many women feel at war with their bodies, and what changes when they stop fighting.
  • Eating disorders: what to notice and how to help. Warning signs, what not to say, and how to support a friend, student or sister.
  • Trauma and the nervous system. How the body protects us, and why understanding that changes everything.
  • Self-abandonment and people-pleasing. The quiet ways we learn to disappear, and how to come back.
  • Shame, grief and sacred anger. The feelings we are taught to hide, and what they are trying to tell us.
  • Burnout in caregivers and helping professionals. Holding others without losing yourself.

Giving Back

Art That Stays With Survivors

Beyond workshops, I have donated original artwork to Safe Alliance’s fundraising auctions to support survivors of domestic violence, and one of my pieces now hangs in a space where survivors begin to heal. Creative work has always been part of how I help people come back to themselves, and it matters to me that it can do that even when I am not in the room.
