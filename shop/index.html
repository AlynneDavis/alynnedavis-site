The Journal

Soul Nourishment

365 Days to Heal Your Relationship With Food and Self

This journal was created for the woman who wants a kinder, more connected, and more truthful relationship with food, her body, and her inner world. Written and designed by Alynne Davis, LCMHC, Expressive Arts Therapist, it blends trauma-informed guidance, daily intentions, expressive writing, and gentle self-inquiry.

Each of the 365 pages invites you to slow down, reconnect, and nourish the parts of you that have long needed softness and safety. Whether you are healing from disordered eating, emotional eating, body image struggles, or chronic self-criticism, this journal will meet you with warmth, compassion, and depth.

  • Daily Morning Intention, a grounding practice to begin each day with clarity and connection
  • Daily Healing Prompt, trauma-informed questions exploring the emotional and somatic patterns beneath your relationship with food
  • Evening Reflection, a gentle space to process your day and integrate your insights
  • Notes & Journaling Space, room for expressive arts, deeper writing, and reflective practice
Paperback, Black & White Interior Paperback, Color Interior Hardcover

All three are available on Amazon and contain the same 365 days of guided healing content. The difference is only in the printing and binding, so choose the one you can picture yourself actually keeping by your bed.

Healing & Process Art

Original Art for the Soul

The artwork I create is not separate from my therapeutic work, it is an extension of it. Process art, expressive arts, and healing imagery have been part of my own journey and my clients' journeys for as long as I have been a therapist.

I sell original pieces and prints on Etsy, art made in the spirit of healing, softness, and the kind of beauty that does not demand anything of you. Each piece carries something of the work I do every day.
