Eating Disorder Therapy in Fort Mill, SC

Licensed in both Carolinas, so living on the South Carolina side does not put you out of reach. Online across South Carolina, or in person just up I-77.

The Licensure Problem Nobody Warns You About

If you live in Fort Mill and have looked for an eating disorder therapist in Charlotte, you may have run into something frustrating: you find someone whose work resonates, you reach out, and you learn they can only see clients in North Carolina. The state line runs right through your search results.

I am licensed in both North Carolina and South Carolina. That means Fort Mill, Tega Cay, Rock Hill, Indian Land, and the rest of York and Lancaster County are all within reach, whether we meet online or you drive up to the Charlotte office.

It is a small detail that turns out to matter enormously when you have finally worked up the nerve to reach out and do not want to start over.

Two Ways to Work Together

Online, from home

Most Fort Mill clients I see meet entirely by secure video. No I-77 at rush hour, no finding parking, no building an extra hour into an already full day. You log on from your own space, which many women find makes the harder conversations easier rather than harder.

In person in Charlotte

The office is in south Charlotte near Ballantyne, roughly 20 to 30 minutes from most of Fort Mill. Some women want the separation of leaving the house and driving somewhere that is only theirs. If that is you, in-person sessions are available.

What We Work On

Binge eating, emotional eating, restriction, bulimia, orthorexia, chronic dieting, and the body image distress that usually sits underneath all of it. You do not need a formal diagnosis, and you do not need to be "sick enough." Most women who come to me have been managing something quietly for years while everyone around them assumed they were fine.

My approach is holistic, which means we look at sleep, nourishment, movement, relationships, and stress alongside the eating itself, because those are rarely separate problems. It also draws on somatic work and creative expression, because some of what you are carrying does not have words attached to it yet.

You can read more about how I work with eating disorders, or about binge eating and restriction specifically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you see clients who live in Fort Mill, SC?

Yes. I am licensed in both North Carolina and South Carolina, so I can work with you by telehealth anywhere in South Carolina, or in person at the Charlotte office.

How far is the Charlotte office from Fort Mill?

The office is in south Charlotte near Ballantyne, roughly 20 to 30 minutes from most of Fort Mill depending on traffic and where you are starting from.

Do I have to come in person?

No. Many Fort Mill clients are seen entirely by secure telehealth. In-person sessions are available if you prefer them.

What if I am not sure it is "bad enough"?

That thought is one of the most common reasons women wait years longer than they needed to. You do not need a diagnosis or a crisis to deserve support.

Do you take insurance?

This is primarily a private-pay practice, with superbills available for out-of-network reimbursement. You can read the full details on the therapy page.
