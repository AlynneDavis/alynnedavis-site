Embodied Reclamation Studio  ·  Personal Growth & Coaching

The Embodied Reclamation Series

Immersive coaching experiences for self-discovery, authenticity, and embodied living. Hours instead of minutes, for the woman who is ready to meet herself again.

Schedule a Discovery Call

The Coaching Path

  1. Discovery Call
  2. Embodied Foundations Series
  3. Embodied Self-Discovery Series
  4. Ongoing Coaching

You have spent years being the one who holds it all together.

For your kids, your parents, a partner, a family that needed you to be steady. Maybe it was a relationship where your feelings were too much, so you learned to have fewer of them. Maybe it was a divorce, a loss, an emptier house, or a body that changed, and one day you looked up and did not know who you are when no one needs anything from you.

Nothing about that means you are broken. You adapted. The ways you learned to leave yourself made sense at the time, and they worked, until they started costing more than they gave.

The Embodied Reclamation Series is a focused way to find your way back. It brings together guided reflection, the MARI®, creative and expressive practices, and somatic awareness, grounded in the RRR Method™. It happens in two stages, and you can stop after the first.

Two Stages, One Path

Every Series begins with the Embodied Foundations Series. What comes after is your choice.

The Embodied Foundations Series

Your personalized coaching blueprint

Foundations leans into Remember: not recovering who you were, but understanding who you became. The beliefs you absorbed, the roles you took on, the quiet agreements about what you had to be in order to belong.

  • An opening session with the MARI®, about 90 minutes, exploring where you are now, the season you are in, and what you are longing for
  • My personal reflection on your MARI®, about two hours I spend between sessions with what emerged, so our next session begins from a considered picture of you
  • A two-hour blueprint session, where we look at what surfaced together and shape it into your personalized coaching blueprint: your patterns, your growth intentions, and where you want to go from here

Foundations is complete on its own. Some women take their blueprint into their lives. Some carry it into ongoing coaching. Many choose to continue into the Self-Discovery Series.

The Embodied Self-Discovery Series

Where understanding becomes something you live

The Self-Discovery Series is three more two-hour sessions that build on your blueprint. It leans into Reclaim: choosing, in your body as much as your mind, what you carry forward and what you set down.

  • Two sessions of experiential practice with the parts and patterns your blueprint named, through creative expression, somatic awareness, and guided reflection
  • A personalized integration session, with a plan for what comes next, whether that is ongoing coaching or carrying the work forward on your own
  • A companion self-discovery journal to support you between sessions and long after

The Self-Discovery Series follows Foundations and is best begun while what you discovered is still fresh. If you continue within 30 days, it is offered at a reduced rate.

And through both stages, you are returning. Return is not a step in the middle. It is what happens the whole way through, each time you come back to yourself in the room.

Included With the Self-Discovery Series

Your Companion Self-Discovery Journal

Designed to support reflection, deepen self-awareness, and help you integrate your discoveries between sessions and long after the Series ends.

The goal isn’t to become someone different. It’s to deepen your relationship with who you are.

About the MARI®

The MARI® (Mandala Assessment Research Instrument) is a research-based creative tool that uses color, symbol, and imagery to reach parts of your inner world that words alone may not. In the Series it is used for guided self-exploration, not diagnosis. No artistic skill is needed, only a willingness to explore.

Is This You?

The Series may be a good fit if you:

  • Have spent years caring for everyone else and are no longer sure what you need
  • Are finding your footing after a relationship that made you smaller
  • Are moving through a life transition, such as divorce, loss, children leaving home, or midlife, and do not recognize yourself in it
  • Look fine on the outside and feel disconnected on the inside
  • Want focused, uninterrupted time for this work rather than fitting it in week by week
  • Are a clinician or helping professional seeking your own deep personal work

In the Spirit of Honesty

A Coaching Experience, Not Therapy

The Embodied Reclamation Series is personal growth and coaching. It does not diagnose or treat mental health conditions, and it is not a substitute for therapy. It is not the right fit if you are in crisis, need medical or psychiatric care, or are looking for treatment for an eating disorder, trauma, or another mental health concern.

If what you are carrying calls for clinical support, my therapeutic intensives and therapy are offered separately, for women in North Carolina and South Carolina. Not sure which fits? That is exactly what the discovery call is for. If you are in crisis, please call or text 988.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I do the Embodied Foundations Series on its own?

Yes. Foundations is complete on its own and ends with your personalized coaching blueprint. Some women take it into their lives or into ongoing coaching, and many continue into the Self-Discovery Series. If you continue within 30 days, the Self-Discovery Series is offered at a reduced rate.

Do I have to do Foundations before ongoing coaching?

No. Foundations is required before the Self-Discovery Series, but you can begin Embodied Reclamation Coaching directly. We will talk about which starting point fits you on your discovery call.

How is the Series different from a therapy intensive?

They serve different purposes. Therapeutic intensives are psychotherapy, for addressing mental health concerns such as eating disorders, body image, and trauma. The Embodied Reclamation Series is coaching, for self-discovery, personal growth, and embodied living. Both honor the connection between mind, body, and lived experience.

Is the Series available online?

Yes. The Series is offered in person in Charlotte, NC, or virtually wherever you live. Scheduling is flexible and arranged together around your availability.

Do I need artistic skill for the MARI or the creative practices?

No. The MARI® uses color, symbol, and imagery to reach what words alone may not. No artistic skill is needed, only a willingness to explore.
