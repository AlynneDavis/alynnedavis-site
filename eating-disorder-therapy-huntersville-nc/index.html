Eating Disorder Therapy in Huntersville, NC

For women around Lake Norman who are quietly struggling with food and their bodies. Online across North Carolina, or in person in Charlotte.

You Do Not Have to Drive to Uptown

Most of the eating disorder specialists near Huntersville are clustered in Charlotte proper, which means a drive south on I-77 at exactly the hour it is worst. For a woman already stretched thin, holding a job and a household together, that drive is often the thing that quietly ends the search.

It does not have to be part of it. Most of the women I see around Lake Norman, in Huntersville, Cornelius, Davidson, and Mooresville, meet with me entirely by secure video from home. The care is the same. The commute disappears.

Two Ways to Work Together

Online, from home

Secure video sessions anywhere in North Carolina. No drive, no parking deck, no extra hour built into the day. Many women find it easier to say the hard things from their own space than from an unfamiliar room.

In person in Charlotte

The office is in south Charlotte near Ballantyne. It is a real drive from Lake Norman, so most Huntersville clients choose telehealth. But if leaving the house is part of what makes it feel like yours, the option is there.

What We Work On

Binge eating, emotional eating, restriction, bulimia, orthorexia, chronic dieting, and the body image distress underneath. You do not need a diagnosis, and you do not need to have hit some threshold of bad enough. Most women I work with have been managing quietly for years while everyone assumed they were fine.

The approach is holistic, looking at sleep, nourishment, movement, relationships, and stress alongside the eating itself, since those are rarely separate problems. It also draws on somatic work and creative expression.

You can read more about how I work with eating disorders, or about binge eating and body image specifically.

Alynne Davis, LCMHC offers eating disorder therapy in person in Charlotte, NC and via telehealth throughout NC and SC — including Huntersville, Cornelius, Davidson, and Mooresville, plus Raleigh, Asheville, Durham, Wilmington, Columbia SC, and Greenville SC.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you see clients in Huntersville, NC?

Yes. I work with women throughout the Lake Norman area, including Huntersville, Cornelius, Davidson, and Mooresville, by telehealth anywhere in North Carolina or in person in Charlotte.

Do I have to drive to Charlotte?

No. Most Huntersville clients meet entirely by telehealth, which avoids the drive south on I-77.

What eating concerns do you work with?

Binge eating, emotional eating, restriction, bulimia, orthorexia, chronic dieting, and body image distress. No diagnosis required.

How quickly can I be seen?

It starts with a free 15-minute consultation. Send a short message about what you are looking for and I will follow up to find a time.

Do you take insurance?

This is primarily a private-pay practice, with superbills available for out-of-network reimbursement. Full details are on the therapy page.
