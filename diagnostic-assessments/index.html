A Diagnosis Shouldn't Take a Year to Get

You already know something isn't adding up. What you don't have is six months to wait for an appointment, a referral, and another referral after that, just to find out. You want an answer, and you want to get it somewhere that feels safe, not clinical and cold.

Schedule a Free Consultation

An Easier Way to Get the Answers You're Looking For

Through my partnership with Mentavi Health, I'm able to offer clients direct access to online diagnostic assessments for ADHD, anxiety, depression, and OCD, without the wait and without being referred out to someone you've never met. You complete it entirely online, at your own pace, from home, in whatever environment feels safest to you.

Every evaluation is reviewed by a licensed clinical psychologist and grounded in peer-reviewed research published in The Journal of Clinical Psychiatry. This isn't a quiz you find online and forget about. It's a real diagnostic evaluation, just without the months-long waitlist standing between you and an answer.

What This Assessment Can Help With

This evaluation is best suited for the following, and can be especially helpful if you need documentation for accommodations or medication management:

ADHD

A formal evaluation for adults whose attention, focus, or executive function challenges have never been named, or who suspect a childhood diagnosis was missed entirely.

Anxiety

Clarity on whether what you're carrying is generalized anxiety, something more specific, or part of a broader picture that's easier to treat once it has a name.

Depression

An evaluation that looks at the full clinical picture, not just a checklist of symptoms, to determine what's actually happening and what kind of support fits.

OCD

A diagnostic evaluation for obsessive-compulsive patterns that often go unrecognized or are mistaken for anxiety or perfectionism alone.

How It Works

  • Complete the assessment online, entirely at your own pace and on your own schedule, from home
  • A licensed clinical psychologist reviews your results and clinical context to determine a diagnosis where warranted
  • You receive a full report, along with a superbill you can submit to your insurance for out-of-network reimbursement

Cost and Access

The assessment is $219, or three payments of $73. Clients currently working with me receive a discount code, ask about access at your next session.

Once you have your code, you can start your assessment through Mentavi Health. A dedicated page for my practice is in the works and will replace this link once it's live.

Frequently Asked Questions

What conditions can this assessment help diagnose?

This online diagnostic assessment, offered through my partnership with Mentavi Health, is best suited for ADHD, anxiety, depression, and OCD. A licensed clinical psychologist reviews your results and determines a diagnosis where warranted.

How does the online assessment process work?

You complete the evaluation entirely online, at your own pace, from home. A licensed clinical psychologist reviews your responses and clinical context to determine a diagnosis where warranted, and you receive a full report along with a superbill you can submit to your insurance for out-of-network reimbursement.

How much does the assessment cost?

The assessment is $219, or three payments of $73. Clients currently working with me receive a discount code, available by asking at your next session.

Is this assessment covered by insurance?

The assessment is not billed directly to insurance, but you receive a superbill after completing it, which you can submit to your insurance provider for out-of-network reimbursement.

Do I have to be a current client to access this assessment?

Access is intended for clients currently working with me. Ask about your access and discount code at your next session.

Can this assessment diagnose an eating disorder?

This assessment is best suited for ADHD, anxiety, depression, and OCD. If you're navigating an eating disorder or body image concern, that's work we can do together directly in our sessions.
